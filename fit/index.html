Moods

Fit a mood to your level

Every level is different. The Fit tab shifts a mood to suit yours, and Apply to Level saves what you set with the level.

Fit to this level

Four sliders shift the mood without changing it. Try three of them below, on real pictures from the Mood Lab taken at three values each.

0.0

SliderRangeStarts atWhat it does
Brightness-3 to +3 stops0Shifts the whole picture, like exposure. The lights keep their strength.
Level lamps-3 to +3 stops0Makes your level's own lamps stronger or weaker, while the rest stays about the same.
Fog0% to 300%100%Multiplies the mood's fog. 0% is clear air.
Surface wear0% to 150%100%Multiplies the dirt, damp and wet floors. 0% shows your level's own surfaces.

Reset puts the four sliders back. In a game, the Set Mood Adjustments node sets the same four values.

Weather

The Weather list and its sliders sit under the four sliders. They are explained in Weather.

Sun and moon

Each mood picks where its light comes from. To keep one direction in every mood, aim it yourself.

SliderRangeStarts atWhat it does
Turn0° to 360°0°The direction the light comes from, around the level. Moving it ticks Aim it myself.
Height0° to 90°30°The angle above the horizon. Low light reaches far along the floor.
  • Aim it myself ticks itself as soon as you move a slider. The numbers show degrees.
  • Your aim stays with every mood you click, until you untick Aim it myself.
  • Use my level's sun turns the light to where your level's own Directional Light points.
  • With no aim, the moon rises in the direction you were looking when you clicked your first mood, so light comes toward you and the wet floor reflects it.

Parts

Each mood is made of parts. Untick any part your level already does well, and your level keeps its own version of it. Point at a part in the panel to read what it does.

SkyThe mood's sky with its moon, stars and clouds. Off keeps your own sky.
Sun and moon lightThe mood's sun or moon light. Off keeps your own directional light.
Branch shadowsMoving shadows of trees that are not in your level, for forests and parks. Off at first. Cloud shadows stay either way.
Sky lightThe soft light from the whole sky. Off keeps your own sky light.
Height fogThe mood's fog. Off keeps your own height fog.
Fog bankLow fog that rolls along the ground. It needs the height fog on.
Surface wearDirt, damp, wet floors, puddles and settled snow or ash on everything.
Dust and weatherDust and embers in the air, and the weather: rain, snow and ash, and what it leaves on the ground.
Lens effectsRain on the lens, old tape, heat shimmer and dark edges.
Colour gradeThe mood's colours, contrast, grain and bloom. Off keeps only its exposure.
Level lampsThe mood dims, tints and breaks the lamps of your level.
Added lampsSome moods bring pools of light of their own to levels with fewer than three lamps and no Mood Lamps.
Player lightThe torch or faint glow some moods give the player.
Branch shadows start unticked

Moving shadows of trees that are not in your level only suit forests and parks, so this part is off until you want it. Cloud shadows stay either way.

In a game, the Set Mood Parts node switches the same parts, and Set Mood Sun Aim aims the light.

Keep it

Apply to Level saves the sliders, weather, parts, sun aim and wind with the level, on the BP_MoodDirector actor. To keep what you set as a mood of your own, for any level, use Save as My Mood.
