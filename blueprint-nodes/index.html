Blueprint nodes

Moods are ordinary data assets, so any Blueprint can use them. Right-click in a graph and type Mood to find these nodes.

Open the gallery once

The nodes appear after the Mood Gallery has been opened once in the session. If a node is missing from the list, open the gallery and try again.

Example: a mood when the game starts

◆Event BeginPlay
fSet Mood
Mood DA_BloodMoonBlend Seconds 2.0
Target is BPL Mood Kit
  1. Open the Level Blueprint. In the toolbar, click Blueprints, then Open Level Blueprint.
  2. Add Event BeginPlay if it is not there. Drag from its white arrow, type Set Mood and pick Set Mood.
  3. Pick a mood in the Mood box, such as DA_BloodMoon. Click Compile and press Play. The game fades from dark into Blood Moon.

If your Set Mood node also has a Target input, you picked the one on the mood actor. Delete it and pick the other one.

Change the mood

fSet Mood
Mood NoneBlend Seconds 2.0
Target is BPL Mood Kit
Set Mood. Changes the mood of the level. Blend Seconds fades from the current look; 0 changes at once.
fClear Mood
Blend Seconds 2.0
Target is BPL Mood Kit
Clear Mood. Returns the level to how it was built.
fGet Mood Director
Director
Target is BPL Mood Kit
Get Mood Director. Finds the mood actor in this level, and creates one if there is none.

Fit it to the level

fSet Mood Adjustments
Brightness 0.0Lights 0.0Fog 1.0Surface Wear 1.0
Target is BPL Mood Kit
Set Mood Adjustments. Fits the mood to this level: Brightness and Lights in stops, Fog and Surface Wear as multipliers.
fSet Mood Parts
Sky Sun And Moon Light Branch Shadows Sky Light Height Fog Fog Bank Surface Wear Dust And Weather Lens Effects Colour Grade Level Lamps Added Lamps Player Light
Target is BPL Mood Kit
Set Mood Parts. Switches parts of the mood on or off. A part that is off leaves the level's own version of it.
fSet Mood Sun Aim
By Hand Turn 0.0Height 30.0
Target is BPL Mood Kit
Set Mood Sun Aim. Aims the sun or moon by hand. Turn is the Z rotation of the light, Height the angle above the horizon. By Hand off lets each mood choose.

Weather

fSet Mood Weather
Weather NoneBlend Seconds 5.0
Target is BPL Mood Kit
Set Mood Weather. Puts a weather on the mood: rain, snow, ash or clear skies. Leave Weather empty for the mood's own. Blend Seconds fades it in, and the ground then soaks, dries or gathers snow over time.
fSet Mood Weather Amounts
Rain 1.0Snow And Ash 1.0Dust 1.0Lightning 1.0
Target is BPL Mood Kit
Set Mood Weather Amounts. Turns the weather's rain, its falling snow and ash and its lightning, and the mood's dust and embers, up or down. 1 is as they come, 0 is none.
fSet Mood Wind
Strength 1.0Turn 19.3
Target is BPL Mood Kit
Set Mood Wind. Scales the strength of the wind, where 1 is as the weather has it, and turns it. Turn is the way it blows, in degrees around the level.
fTrigger Lightning
Target is BPL Mood Kit
Trigger Lightning. Strikes lightning now: a flash over the level and, in the mood's sky, a bolt. It works in any weather while a mood is on. Strikes stay at least 4 seconds apart, and flash at most 3 times a second.

Lights

fTrigger Blackout
Seconds 3.0
Target is BPL Mood Kit
Trigger Blackout. Cuts the lights for a few seconds, then brings them back. It needs a mood to be active.
fSet Mood Lamp Group
Group NoneOn
Target is BPL Mood Kit
Set Mood Lamp Group. Switches every Mood Lamp whose Group is this on or off. Leave Group empty for every Mood Lamp.
fFlicker Mood Lamps
Group NoneSeconds 3.0
Target is BPL Mood Kit
Flicker Mood Lamps. Makes every Mood Lamp whose Group is this flicker for a few seconds. Leave Group empty for every Mood Lamp.
fSwing Mood Lamps
Group NoneDegrees 12.0
Target is BPL Mood Kit
Swing Mood Lamps. Knocks every hanging Mood Lamp whose Group is this, so it swings and slowly settles. Leave Group empty for every Mood Lamp.
fRefresh Mood Lights
Target is BPL Mood Kit
Refresh Mood Lights. Call this after streaming in a level, so its lamps join the current mood.
