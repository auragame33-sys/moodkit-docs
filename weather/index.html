Weather

Weather sits on top of any mood. The mood keeps its light, fog and colours, and the weather brings what falls, what it leaves on the ground, and the wind.

Put a weather on a mood

Every mood comes with a weather of its own: Drowned brings Rain, Blind Fog brings Light Ash and Ash Fall brings Ash. The others bring Clear. You can put any weather on any mood.

  • In the editor: on the Fit tab, pick from the Weather list. Mood's own keeps the one the mood comes with, and the line under the list says what it is. Your pick stays on every mood you click, until you pick Mood's own again. Apply to Level saves it.
  • From a Blueprint: Set Mood Weather, with Blend Seconds to fade it in.
  • When the player walks in: a Mood Event's New Weather, or a Weather Zone.

The ground builds up

Rain soaks the floors and fills puddles over time, and they dry slowly after it stops. Snow and ash settle on everything that faces up, and melt away when the weather changes.

  • In a game this takes minutes, at the pace the weather sets. In the editor it runs ten times faster, so a preview shows it.
  • A game starts with the ground as if the weather had been going for a while.
  • Snow and ash settle only on what faces up, such as floors, the tops of crates and the tops of walls. Snow stays out of your rooms; ash lets a little drift in.
  • Wet floors and settled snow are part of Surface wear. With that part unticked, they are gone too.

One wind for everything

Each weather has a wind of its own, from a breeze at 1 to a gale at 3. The Wind slider scales it and Direction turns it, and Set Mood Wind does the same in a game. A hanging lamp inside an interior volume keeps the swing it was given, because rooms keep the wind out.

Turn it up or down

SliderRangeStarts atWhat it does
Rain0% to 200%100%The weather's rain. Also turns down the lens drops and puddle rings.
Snow and ash0% to 200%100%The weather's falling snow or ash.
Dust and embers0% to 200%100%The mood's own dust and embers.
Lightning0% to 200%100%How often lightning strikes. 0% stops it; Test Lightning still works.
Wind0% to 200%100%The strength of the weather's wind.
Direction0° to 360°19.3°The way the wind blows, in degrees around the level.

Test Lightning strikes at once, so you can judge a storm without waiting for one. Reset under the weather sliders brings back the mood's own weather, every slider at 100% and the wind's usual direction. In a game, Set Mood Weather Amounts sets the same amounts.

Make your own weather

A weather is an asset, so a new one needs no code.

  1. Duplicate a weather from MoodKit > Core > Weather, such as WX_Snow.
  2. Move the copy into a folder named MyWeather inside MoodKit.
  3. Open it and change it. Its groups are Card, Rain, Snow and Ash, Ground, Build Up, Lightning and Wind, and each value says what it does.
  4. Open the panel again. Your weather appears in the list.

Every value is listed in Weather values.
