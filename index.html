A mood changes the whole world together.

Each mood is a complete look: sky, light, fog, surfaces, air and camera, tuned to work as one. Click a card and it blends in on your level in about a second.

Same corridor. Another world.

Nothing in the level was edited: no mesh, no material. Every change comes from the mood, and Back to Original brings your level back.

What you get

Everything a scene needs, and none of it in your way.

Every part is optional, every value has help text, and your own moods, weathers and lamp styles live apart from the pack, so an update never touches them.

How it works

Pick. Fit. Apply.

The Mood Gallery docks at the side of the editor. Nothing changes in your level until you click Apply.

Pick a mood

Click a card and see it on your level at once.

Fit it to your level

Sliders, weather, sun aim and 13 parts you can switch off.

Place and apply

Add lamps, rooms and events, then Apply and press Play.

Weather on any mood

9 weathers that leave their mark.

Put any weather on any mood. Floors soak, puddles grow, and snow and ash settle on everything that faces up, then dry or melt away again.

Clear
Nothing falls. Wet ground dries and snow melts.
Drizzle
A fine, steady rain. Floors darken slowly.
Rain
Cold rain on standing water. Floors soak and puddles grow.
Storm
Driving rain, a strong gusting wind, and lightning.
Dry Lightning
Lightning flickers in silent clouds, and no rain falls.
Snow
Soft snow drifts down and settles on everything that faces up.
Blizzard
Thick snow blown sideways by a howling wind.
Ash
Dark ash falls from a burning sky and greys the ground.
Light Ash
Pale ash drifts down slowly through still air.
