Mood Events and zones

Boxes that change things when the player walks in. Add any of them from the Place tab, or drag one from MoodKit > Core, then scale it.

Mood Events

BP_MoodTrigger, the Mood Event, does what its tick boxes say when the player walks in. One box can do several things.

  • Flicker a group of Mood Lamps for a few seconds, or switch them on or off.
  • Knock hanging lamps so they swing and slowly settle.
  • Start a blackout. Flame and Alarm lamps stay on, because a flame has no power to cut.
  • Strike lightning, in any weather.
  • Change the mood or the weather, with a blend.
  • Wait a delay first, and happen only once if you like.

Mood Event (BP_MoodTrigger)

ValueRangeDefaultWhat it does
Only OnceswitchtickedHappens only the first time the player walks in.
Delay Secondsnumber0 to 100Waits this long after the player walks in.
GroupnameemptyWhich Mood Lamps flicker or switch: those with this Group. Empty means every Mood Lamp.
Flicker Secondsnumber0 to 103The lamps flicker for this long. 0 is no flicker.
Swing Degreesnumber0 to 300Hanging lamps of the group swing this far, as if something knocked them, then slowly settle. 0 is no swing.
Switch LampsswitchuntickedSwitches the lamps on or off. With Flicker too, they flicker first.
Lamps OnswitchuntickedWith Switch Lamps: on is ticked, off is unticked.
BlackoutswitchuntickedCuts the lights that follow the mood. Flame and Alarm lamps stay on.
Blackout Secondsnumber0 to 304How long the blackout lasts.
LightningswitchuntickedStrikes lightning as the player walks in, in any weather.
New Moodmood assetemptyChanges to this mood. Leave empty to keep the mood.
Mood Secondsnumber0 to 102How long the change of mood takes.
New Weatherweather assetemptyChanges the weather to this one, such as rain or snow. Leave empty to keep the weather.
Weather Secondsnumber0 to 6010How long the change of weather takes. The ground goes on soaking, drying or gathering snow for longer, at the pace the weather sets.
Events fire in the game

A Mood Event only fires when the player walks in during play. In the editor nothing happens.

Mood Zones

BP_MoodZone changes the mood while the player is inside it, and puts the earlier mood back as the player leaves.

Mood Zone (BP_MoodZone)

ValueRangeDefaultWhat it does
Moodmood assetemptyThe mood while the player is inside. Leave empty to return the level to its own look.
Blend Secondsnumber2How long the change takes.
Restore On ExitswitchtickedGoes back to the earlier mood when the player leaves.

Weather Zones

BP_MoodWeatherZone changes the weather while the player is inside it. More in Storms and lightning.

Weather Zone (BP_MoodWeatherZone)

ValueRangeDefaultWhat it does
Weatherweather assetemptyThe weather while the player is inside, such as WX_Snow or WX_Storm. Leave empty for the mood's own weather.
Blend Secondsnumber0 to 305How long the change of weather takes.
Restore On ExitswitchtickedGoes back to the earlier weather when the player leaves.
Ground At OnceswitchuntickedThe ground takes this weather's look at once, as if it had always been here, instead of soaking or turning white over time.

In the Mood Lab

  • BasementFlicker is a Mood Event in the basement. The two basement lamps flicker for 3 seconds and swing as if something knocked them. The candle keeps burning, because it has no group.
  • StormCorner is a Weather Zone in a corner of the courtyard. A storm rolls in over 5 seconds.

From a Blueprint, each of these is a single node. See Blueprint nodes.
