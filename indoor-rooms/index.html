Indoor rooms

A level can be indoors and outdoors at once. Mood Kit cannot know where your roofs are, so you show it with interior volumes.

On this page

  2. What stays outside
  3. A room's own light and fog
  4. Good to know

Add an interior volume

  1. Look at the room in the 3D view.
  2. Click Add Interior Volume on the Place tab. A blue box appears where you look, already selected.
  3. Scale it to fit the room: press R in the 3D view and drag a handle. Ctrl+Z removes it again.

You can also drag BP_MoodInterior from MoodKit > Core. The line under the button counts the volumes in your level.

What stays outside

Inside a volume there is no rain, no rain marks or puddle rings, no drops on the lens, and no falling or settled snow. The wind does not reach hanging lamps, and the fog bank stays out. Dust in the air stays, and ash lets a little drift in.

A room's own light and fog

While the view is inside, a room can have its own brightness and fog, the way eyes adjust to a dark room. Select the volume and set its values under Interior.

Interior volume (BP_MoodInterior)

ValueRangeDefaultWhat it does
Brightnessnumber-3 to 30Shifts the exposure while the view is inside, in stops. Positive is brighter, as eyes adjust to a dark room.
Fog Amountnumber0 to 21Multiplies the height fog while the view is inside. 1 is as the mood has it, 0 is clear air.
Keep Rain OutswitchtickedNo rain, rain marks, lens drops, or falling ash and snow inside. Dust in the air stays.
Keep Fog Bank OutswitchtickedNo low fog bank inside.
Blend Metersnumber0 to 51How far the change in brightness and fog spreads either side of the walls, in metres.

Good to know

  • Rain, snow and fog banks stop at the first 8 rooms. More rooms still change brightness and fog.
  • Lightning lights a room only through its doors and windows.
  • On your own levels, rain comes indoors until you add volumes.
  • The Mood Lab has three: the corridor, the side room and the basement.
