Lamp values

What you can set on each placed Mood Lamp, and every value of a lamp style. The style defaults shown are those of Hanging.

Mood Lamp, on each placed lamp

ValueRangeDefaultWhat it does
Stylelamp style assetHow the lamp looks and shines. Pick any Lamp Style, or make your own with Customize This Style on the Place tab.
Brightnessnumber0 to 41Multiplies how bright the lamp is, with or without a mood.
Colourcolour1, 1, 1Multiplies the style's colour. White keeps it.
Reachnumber0.25 to 41Multiplies how far the light reaches.
Cone Degreesnumber0 to 1600How wide the cone of light is. 0 keeps the style's cone.
Stem Metersnumber0 to 120The length of the cable, arm or pole, in metres. 0 keeps the style's length.
Cast ShadowsswitchtickedThe light casts shadows. Off is cheaper when there are many lamps.
Show FixtureswitchtickedShows the lamp itself. Off leaves only the light, for a lamp mesh of your own.
Fog Glownumber0 to 41Multiplies how strongly its light shows in fog.
Sway Degreesnumber0 to 300How far a hanging lamp swings either way, in degrees. 0 keeps it still. It swings in the game, and in the editor while a mood is previewed. Outside an interior volume it swings along the wind, further in a strong wind.
Sway Secondsnumber1 to 103How long one swing of a hanging lamp takes, in seconds.
GroupnameemptyA name for switching lamps together. A Mood Event or the Set Mood Lamp Group node switches every lamp with this group.
Starts OffswitchuntickedThe lamp starts switched off, until a Mood Event or Set Mood Lamp Group switches it on.

Lamp style: Style

ValueRangeDefaultWhat it does
Display NametextemptyThe name of this style.

Lamp style: Light

ValueRangeDefaultWhat it does
All RoundswitchuntickedShines all round, like a bare bulb or a flame. Off shines a cone, like a shaded lamp.
Cone Degreesnumber10 to 160110How wide the cone of light is, in degrees.
Aim Pitchnumber-90 to 90-90Where the cone points: -90 is straight down, 0 is straight ahead.
Keynumber0 to 102.6How brightly the lamp lights what is in front of it while a mood runs. 1 is a soft pool, 3 a bright one. The mood dims or brightens it further.
Editor Candelanumber0 to 5000350How bright the lamp is when no mood is on, in candelas.
Reach Metersnumber1 to 6010How far its light reaches, in metres.
Colourcolour1, 0.82, 0.62The colour of its light.
Soft Shadowsnumber0 to 504The size of the light source in centimetres. Bigger gives softer shadows.
Fog Glownumber0 to 41How strongly its light shows in fog.
Is FlameswitchFlickers like a flame and keeps its own colour in every mood, unless its role is set otherwise.
Is AlarmswitchPulses like an alarm and keeps its own colour in every mood, unless its role is set otherwise.

Lamp style: Placing

ValueRangeDefaultWhat it does
Hangs DownswitchtickedHangs from the ceiling on its stem.
On WallswitchuntickedFixed to a wall, with its stem pointing out. With neither, it stands on the floor or a table.
Stem Metersnumber0 to 120.7The length of the cable, arm or pole, in metres.
Stem Widthnumber0 to 401.2The thickness of the cable, arm or pole, in centimetres.

Lamp style: Meshes

ValueRangeDefaultWhat it does
Stem Meshstatic meshCylinderThe cable, arm or pole. It is stretched to the stem length, so a mesh one metre long fits best.
Body Meshstatic meshConeThe shade, housing or candle.
Body Sizex, y, z46, 46, 26The size of the body, in centimetres, for a mesh one metre across.
Body Offsetx, y, z0, 0, -13Where the body sits, from the end of the stem.
Body Colourcolour0.045, 0.045, 0.05The colour of the body and the stem.
Glow Meshstatic meshSphereThe bulb, flame or glass that glows.
Glow Sizex, y, z9, 9, 9The size of the glowing part, in centimetres, for a mesh one metre across.
Glow Offsetx, y, z0, 0, -24Where the glowing part sits, from the end of the stem.
Glow Strengthnumber0 to 104How bright the glowing part looks.
Light Offsetx, y, z0, 0, -30Where the light itself sits, from the end of the stem. Keep it outside the body, or the body blocks it.
