Every value

Card

ValueRangeDefaultWhat it does
Display NametextemptyThe name in the panel.
SummarytextemptyOne line about the weather, shown in the panel.
Orderwhole number100Where it sits in the list. Lower comes first.

Rain

ValueRangeDefaultWhat it does
Rain Amountnumber0 to 10How hard it rains. Rain stays out of interior volumes.
Rain Lengthnumber0.1 to 10.5Length of the rain streaks.
Rain Colorcolour0.6, 0.7, 0.8Colour of the rain.
Lens Rainnumber0 to 10Rain drops running down the lens. They stop while the view is inside an interior volume.

Snow and Ash

ValueRangeDefaultWhat it does
Flake Amountnumber0 to 10How much snow or ash falls.
Flake Sizenumber0.2 to 31Size of each flake.
Flake Fallnumber0 to 30.8How fast flakes fall, in metres a second.
Flake Driftnumber0 to 20.5How much the wind carries the flakes sideways.
Flake Swirlnumber0 to 10.3How far each flake wanders and circles on its way down.
Flake Tumblenumber0 to 100 gives soft round flakes, like snow. 1 gives flat flakes that turn over as they fall, like ash.
Flake Shadenumber0 to 10.1How much flakes darken what is behind them. Ash darkens it, snow hardly does.
Flake Colorcolour0.9, 0.92, 0.95Colour of the flakes.
Flake Indoorsnumber0 to 10How much also falls and settles inside interior volumes. 0 keeps it all outside.

Ground

ValueRangeDefaultWhat it does
Ground Wetnumber0 to 10How wet floors get while this weather lasts.
Ground Puddlesnumber0 to 10How much standing water gathers.
Ground Ripplesnumber0 to 10Rain rings in puddles, and wet walls. They stay out of interior volumes.
Ground Covernumber0 to 10How much snow or ash settles on everything that faces up.
Cover Colorcolour0.82, 0.84, 0.88Colour of the snow or ash that settles.

Build Up

ValueRangeDefaultWhat it does
Soak Secondsnumber1 to 60060In a game, how long dry ground takes to get as wet as this weather makes it.
Dry Secondsnumber1 to 900240In a game, how long ground that is wetter than this weather leaves it takes to dry.
Settle Secondsnumber1 to 900120In a game, how long snow or ash takes to settle as thick as this weather leaves it.
Melt Secondsnumber1 to 1800400In a game, how long settled snow or ash takes to go when this weather has less of it.

Lightning

ValueRangeDefaultWhat it does
Lightning Per Minutenumber0 to 200How many strikes a minute, on average. 0 is none. Strikes are always at least 4 seconds apart, and each flashes at most 3 times.
Lightning Brightnessnumber0 to 41How brightly a strike lights the level and the sky.
Lightning Boltnumber0 to 10.6How often the bolt itself shows in the sky. The other strikes light the clouds from behind.
Lightning Colorcolour0.75, 0.82, 1Colour of the lightning.

Wind

ValueRangeDefaultWhat it does
Wind Strengthnumber0 to 31How strong the wind is. 1 is a breeze, 3 a gale. Clouds, fog, rain, snow, branch shadows and swinging lamps all follow it.
Wind Gustsnumber0 to 10.2How much the wind comes and goes in gusts.
