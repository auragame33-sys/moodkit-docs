Lamp styles

A lamp style is an asset: its meshes, its light and how it is fixed. There are 8, and you can make your own in one click.

These are each style's own values. Every lamp can still change its brightness, colour, reach, cone and stem in the Details panel.

Make your own style

  1. Select a lamp of the style closest to what you want.
  2. Click Customize This Style on the Place tab, under Your own lamp styles. A copy is made in MoodKit > MyLampStyles, named like Hanging Custom, and the selected lamps take it in one undo.
  3. Change any value in the window that opens. Your lamps follow each change within a second, with or without a mood.
  4. Add more lamps of it with Add Lamp, next to Your style.

With no lamp selected, Customize This Style copies the last style you used. A style that is already yours just opens again.

Use your own meshes

Set the meshes of your style under Meshes.

  • Sizes are in centimetres, for a mesh one metre across, and offsets are measured from the end of the stem.
  • The stem is stretched to the stem length, so a stem mesh one metre long fits best.
  • Keep Light Offset outside the body mesh, or the body blocks the light.
  • Or untick Show Fixture on a lamp and put it inside a lamp mesh of your own.
Your styles follow your changes while the panel is open

With the panel closed, a lamp shows a change to its style once you move it.

Every value is listed in Lamp values.
